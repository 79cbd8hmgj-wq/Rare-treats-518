# Owner content intake

The following information must be supplied or approved before launch.

## Business contact

- Public business email
- Optional public phone number
- Approved Instagram URL
- Optional business Facebook page URL

## Treat menu

- Current category names
- Current example products
- Pricing language or starting prices
- Standard quantities and sizes
- Seasonal availability rules

## Custom orders

- Minimum lead time
- Order minimums
- Deposit requirements
- Accepted payment methods
- Pickup location or general area
- Delivery availability, range, and fees
- Change and cancellation policy

## Food and safety wording

- Approved ingredient-request process
- Approved allergen and cross-contact language
- Any dietary accommodation language Misty is comfortable publishing

## Photography

- Original logo file
- Product photographs approved for website publication
- Market or event photographs approved for website publication
- Owner portrait approved for website publication
- Alt text or identifying context for every image

## Events

For each confirmed event:

- Event name
- Venue
- Street address or approved location label
- Date
- Start and end times
- Event link
- Optional event image or flyer approved for publication

## Launch

- Production URL or custom domain
- Final owner approval of all page wording
- Final owner approval of policies and customer flow

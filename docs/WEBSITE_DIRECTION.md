# Rare Treats 518 — Website direction

## Public scope

The first public version is restricted to homemade baked goods, custom dessert inquiries, gift and seasonal treats, and local markets or pop-ups.

Savory food and catering are intentionally excluded from navigation, page copy, metadata, and customer-facing content. The repository can support those areas later only after Misty defines and approves the service scope.

## Brand source

The visual direction is based on supplied screenshots of Rare Treats 518 branding and social media:

- Iridescent pastel heart logo
- Aqua, blush pink, lavender, cream, and deep purple
- The line “Homemade with Love, Made for You!”
- Owner-led market presentation in Troy, New York
- Cookies, brownies, dessert trays, gift bags, jars, and seasonal specialties

The implementation interprets those cues through a code-native heart mark, pastel gradients, editorial typography, and market-inspired illustration. It does not copy social-media interface screenshots into the public site and does not use fake food photography.

## Customer paths

1. Explore rotating treat categories.
2. Review the information needed for a custom-order inquiry.
3. Message Rare Treats 518 through the confirmed Instagram account.
4. Check Instagram for newly confirmed market dates.

## Foundation status

- No ecommerce checkout
- No online payment collection
- No active website form
- No fixed product pricing
- No shipping promise
- No allergy-safe claim
- No publication-approved product or owner gallery yet
- No public business email or phone yet

The site remains in preview mode and is blocked from search indexing until launch inputs are approved.

# Rare Treats 518 — Website Direction

## Confirmed public scope

The first website version is focused on:

- Homemade desserts and baked treats
- Custom-order inquiries
- Local markets, pop-ups, and community events
- Misty and the Rare Treats 518 story
- Working order information and policies

Savory food and catering are intentionally hidden. They are not present in navigation, customer-facing copy, route structure, or calls to action. They should remain disabled until Misty explicitly confirms that those services belong on the public website.

## Visual interpretation

The supplied social-media and flyer references establish:

- A pastel layered-heart identity
- Aqua, blush pink, lavender, cream, and deep purple
- Friendly handmade signage and market presentation
- A personal, owner-led business rather than a polished national bakery chain
- Strong product variety and seasonal availability

The website refines those cues with disciplined typography, generous spacing, rounded shapes, and real owner-approved photography. It must not invent products, prices, testimonials, events, or business policies.

## Customer paths

1. Explore treat categories.
2. Learn how custom-order requests work.
3. Find upcoming local markets and events.
4. Learn about Misty and follow the business on social media.

## Current limitations

- No direct checkout
- No online payment
- No live inventory
- No published prices
- No form backend
- No confirmed events
- No approved final policies
- No publication-approved original logo or website image set

The temporary SVG heart and CSS artwork are placeholders for a working preview. They are not intended to replace Misty’s original logo or product photography.
